# Support

Need help with Momenthos, found a bug, or have an idea? Email [wesley.momenthos@gmail.com](mailto:wesley.momenthos@gmail.com).

It helps if you include:

- Your device (for example, iPhone 15 or iPad Air)
- Your iOS version (Settings → General → About)
- What you were doing and what happened

The [guide](/guide/getting-started) covers how each part of the app works.

## Common questions

### Do I need an account?

No. Momenthos has no account or sign-in. You choose a display name the first time you open it, and that's all.

### Where is my data stored?

On your device. The app doesn't send your moments anywhere unless you send a package, link a family shelf, or export a backup. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

### How do I move my library to a new phone?

Export a backup in Settings, move the file to your new device (AirDrop, Files or iCloud Drive), then import it in Settings on the new device. Your library is also restored if you restore the whole device from an iCloud or computer backup. See [Backup and import](/guide/backup-and-import).

### I lost my phone. Can you recover my moments?

No. I never have a copy of your data. Your moments can come back from a device backup, an exported backup file, or a family shelf another device still has.

### Someone sent me a .momenthos file. How do I open it?

Tap the file and choose Momenthos, then enter the passphrase the sender gave you. See [Sending a package](/guide/packages).

### I set up a new device and my family shelf won't open.

Shelf keys stay on the device and aren't in backups. Get the key again from another family member's device, or use a key code you saved. See [Family shelves](/guide/family-shelves).

### Does Momenthos cost anything?

No, it's free.
