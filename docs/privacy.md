# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 14, 2026

Momenthos is made by Wesley Geddes ("I", "me"). This policy explains what happens to the information you put into the Momenthos app for iPhone and iPad.

## Summary

Momenthos does not collect your data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. I don't run any servers for the app, and nothing you write is sent to me.

## Information stored on your device

Everything you add to Momenthos (moments, context, people and birthdates, tags, shelves, reflections and feelings, your display name) is stored in a database inside the app on your device.

- It is never sent to me or to any third party by the app.
- It is included in your device's iCloud or computer backups if you have those turned on. Apple handles those backups under its own privacy policy.
- Deleting the app deletes this data from your device.

The app creates a random identifier for your installation and stores it in your device's Keychain. It is used inside the app and for family shelves. It is never sent to me.

## Sharing you choose to do

Momenthos only moves your information off your device when you ask it to.

**Packages.** When you send moments as a package, the app encrypts them with a passphrase you choose and hands the file to the iOS Share Sheet. You decide where it goes (Messages, Mail, AirDrop, Files and so on). Anyone with the file and the passphrase can read it. Reflections are never included in packages.

**Family shelves.** When you link a shelf to a folder, the app writes encrypted copies of that shelf's moments to the iCloud Drive folder you choose. The content is encrypted with a shelf key. People who have access to the folder and the shelf key can read it. Shelf keys are stored only in your device's Keychain and are not included in backups. Apple stores the folder under its iCloud terms and privacy policy. Reflections are never included in family shelves.

**Backups.** When you export a backup, the app creates a JSON file that is **not encrypted**. It can include your reflections if you choose. You decide where to save or send that file.

Removing someone's access to a shared folder stops future changes from reaching them. It cannot remove copies they already have.

## Device features

**Notifications.** If you turn on anniversary reminders, the app schedules them on your device. They contain counts and years, not the text of your moments.

**Siri and Shortcuts.** If you add moments with Siri or Shortcuts, Apple processes your request under its own privacy policy. The moment is saved to your device.

**Writing suggestions.** On devices that support Apple Intelligence, the app can suggest a cleaned-up version of a moment you're writing. This uses Apple's on-device model. Your text is not sent to me.

## Children

Momenthos is meant for adults keeping records of their family, which often includes children. The app does not collect information about anyone. What you write about your family stays on your device unless you share it as described above.
