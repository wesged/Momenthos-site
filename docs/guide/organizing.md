# People, tags and shelves

## People

People are names in your library: your kids, grandparents, friends. Add them while capturing a moment or from Settings.

- **Me** is you. It's created from your display name the first time you open the app.
- Add a **birthdate** to see how old someone was in each moment.
- A person's page shows all of their moments.

## Tags

Tags are short labels such as *funny*, *bedtime* or *first time*. A moment can have any number of tags. Manage them in Settings, and tap a tag anywhere to see its moments.

## Shelves

Shelves group moments. Common setups are one shelf per child, or a shelf for grandparents' stories.

- A moment can be on several shelves, or none.
- Pin shelves you use often.
- Archive a shelf to hide it without deleting anything, and restore it later.
- Deleting a shelf **does not** delete its moments. They stay in your library.

A shelf can also be linked to a shared folder so your family can add to it. See [Family shelves](/guide/family-shelves).
