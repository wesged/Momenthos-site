# Getting started

Momenthos runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later.

## First launch

The first time you open the app, a short tour explains the main ideas. At the end you choose a display name. Momenthos uses it to create **Me**, a person you can attach to moments you said or did yourself. You can change the name later in Settings.

If you have a backup from another device, choose **Enter & import a backup** on the last step. Import is also available any time under **Settings → Import**.

## The main tabs

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Today** | Moments from this date in past years, plus a way into Reflect. |
| **Timeline** | Your whole library in date order, with search and filters. |
| **Threads** | Your library on a calendar, with feelings shown as color. |
| **Shelves** | Groups of moments, such as one shelf per child. Family shelves live here too. |
| **Settings** | Your name, appearance, reminders, people, tags, backup and import. |

## Words you'll see

- **Moment:** something someone said or did, with an optional date, people, tags and context.
- **Person:** someone a moment is about or attributed to. People are just names in your library, not accounts.
- **Shelf:** a group of moments. A moment can be on several shelves or none.
- **Reflection:** a private note on a moment, recording how it felt then or how it feels now.
