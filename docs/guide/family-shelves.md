# Family shelves

A family shelf lets several people add to and read the same shelf over time. It uses a folder in iCloud Drive that you choose, so there's no Momenthos account or server involved.

## How it works

Two things are needed to read a family shelf:

1. **Access to the folder.** You control this with iCloud Drive's folder sharing.
2. **The shelf key.** Momenthos encrypts the shelf's content with this key. Without it, the files in the folder can't be read.

Each device keeps a copy of the shelf and syncs changes through the folder when it's available.

## Setting one up

1. Create an iCloud Drive folder and share it with your family from the Files app.
2. In Momenthos, open a shelf and link it to that folder.
3. Share the shelf key with each family member. You can share it as a QR code, a link or a file.

## Joining one

1. Accept the iCloud Drive folder invitation.
2. In Momenthos, choose to join a shared shelf, pick the folder, and enter or scan the shelf key.

## People on a family shelf

When moments arrive from someone else, Momenthos matches the people in them to people in your library. If it can't tell who someone is, it asks **Who is this?** so you can link them to an existing person or add a new one.

## Conflicts

If two people change the same moment before syncing, Momenthos shows both versions and lets you choose.

## Things to know

- **Keep your shelf key safe.** Keys are stored on the device only and are not in backups. On a new device, get the key again from another family member, or save the key code somewhere secure.
- **Removing access isn't erasing.** Unsharing the folder stops future changes from reaching someone. It can't remove copies they already have.
- **Unlinking keeps your copy.** Unlinking a shelf stops syncing but keeps the moments on your device and leaves the folder alone.
- Reflections are never shared.
