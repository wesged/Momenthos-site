# Family shelves

A family shelf lets several people add to and read the same shelf over time. It syncs through Google Drive, using each person's own Google account, so there's no Momenthos account or server involved. It works between iPhone, iPad and (soon) Android.

## How it works

Two things are needed to read a family shelf:

1. **Access to the shelf in Google Drive.** You invite family members by their Google account email.
2. **The shelf key.** Momenthos encrypts the shelf's content with this key before it leaves your device. Without it, the files in Drive can't be read, not even by Google.

Each device keeps a full copy of the shelf, so it works offline and syncs when you open the app or come back to it.

Momenthos asks Google only for permission to see files it creates or that you pick. It can't see anything else in your Drive.

## Setting one up

1. Open a shelf, then choose **Share with Family…** and sign in with Google. Momenthos creates a "Momenthos" folder in your Drive with the shelf's locked files.
2. Choose **Invite Family…** and enter a family member's Google email. Pick whether they can add moments or only view. Google emails them an invitation.
3. Send them the shelf key. You can share it as a QR code, a link, a file or a code to type. Save the code somewhere safe too, until someone else has the shelf.

## Joining one

1. In Momenthos, go to **Settings → Join Shared Shelf…**, or open the key link or file you were sent.
2. Enter or scan the shelf key, then sign in with Google.
3. Google's picker opens. Choose **Momenthos shelf**. If it isn't listed under Recent, search for it or look in **Shared with me**.

The shelf's moments then appear in your library. Moments you add go into your own Google Drive, so they use your Google storage, not the shelf owner's. Moments are text, so a shelf uses very little space.

## Family members

On a shared shelf, **Family Members…** shows who's on it. The shelf's owner can remove someone. That takes away their Drive access and changes the shelf key. Everyone else picks up the new key automatically the next time they sync. Anyone who can only view needs the new key code sent again.

## Moving a shelf from iCloud

Earlier test versions shared shelves through an iCloud Drive folder. To move one to Google Drive:

1. Make sure everyone has synced their latest changes.
2. The shelf's owner unlinks the iCloud folder (the moments stay on every device), then chooses **Share with Family…** to share it through Google Drive and invites everyone again.
3. Each family member unlinks the old shelf, then joins the new one with the new shelf key.

The old iCloud folder can be deleted once everyone has moved.

## People on a family shelf

When moments arrive from someone else, Momenthos matches the people in them to people in your library. If it can't tell who someone is, it asks **Who is this?** so you can link them to an existing person or add a new one.

## Conflicts

If two people change the same moment before syncing, Momenthos shows both versions and lets you choose.

## Things to know

- **Keep your shelf key safe.** Keys are stored on the device only and are not in backups. On a new device, get the key again from another family member, or use a key code you saved.
- **Removing someone isn't erasing.** It stops future changes from reaching them. It can't remove copies they already have.
- **Unlinking keeps your copy.** Unlinking a shelf stops syncing but keeps the moments on your device and leaves the Drive files alone.
- **Signing out** of Google in Settings → Shared Shelves pauses syncing. Nothing is lost, and syncing picks up when you sign in again.
- Reflections are never shared.
