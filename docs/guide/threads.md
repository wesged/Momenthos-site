# Threads

Threads shows your library on a calendar, so you can see how moments spread out over months and years.

- Each moment shows as a mark. Moments with feelings are colored by feeling.
- Pinch to zoom in and out of time.
- Use the arrange options to group and sort moments, for example by person or shelf.
- Select a thread to read its moments together as a story.

Threads may suggest moments worth reflecting on. Those open in [Reflect](/guide/reflect).
