# Quick capture

Kids say things fast. These shortcuts get you to a new moment with fewer taps.

## From the app icon

Touch and hold the Momenthos icon on your Home Screen and choose **Add Moment**. The app opens straight to a new moment with the keyboard ready.

## With Siri

Momenthos adds two actions to Siri and the Shortcuts app:

- **Add Moment** opens the app to a new moment.
- **Keep Moment** saves a moment from what you say, without opening the app.

Ask Siri, or find them in the Shortcuts app under Momenthos.

## With Shortcuts

Add either action to your own shortcut, or put one on your Home Screen, Lock Screen or the Action button on iPhones that have one.

Moments saved this way appear in your library like any other, and you can add people, tags or a date later.
