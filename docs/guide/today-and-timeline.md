# Today and Timeline

## Today

Today shows moments that happened on this date in earlier years, grouped by how many years ago.

- If nothing happened on this exact date, it shows **This Week in Past Years**: moments within three days either side.
- If that's empty too, it shows a moment **From Your Library**. Tap **Show another** for a different one.
- You can browse a different date and come back to today.

Today is also one way into [Reflect](/guide/reflect).

## Timeline

Timeline is your whole library in date order. Reflections appear on the date you wrote them.

**Search** looks through the words of your moments.

**Filters** narrow the list by:

- Person
- Tag
- Shelf
- Year
- Feeling
- Status

Filters can be combined. You can also switch between when a moment happened and when you added it.

## Moment detail

Tap any moment to open it. You'll see its words, context, people, tags and shelves, along with related moments and any reflections you've written. Tap a person, tag or shelf to see everything else connected to it.
