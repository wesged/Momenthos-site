# Capturing moments

Tap the **+** button on Today, Timeline or Shelves to add a moment.

## What you can add

Only the moment's words are required. Everything else is optional.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Moment** | What was said or what happened. Up to 5,000 characters. |
| **Context** | Background that helps you remember the scene later. Up to 10,000 characters. |
| **Date** | When it happened. Defaults to today and can't be in the future. |
| **People** | Who said or did it. Choose **Me** for yourself. |
| **Tags** | Short labels like *bedtime* or *car rides*. |
| **Shelves** | Which shelves the moment belongs to. |
| **Then feeling** | How the moment felt when it happened. |

If you close the composer with unsaved words, Momenthos asks before discarding them.

## Ages

Add a birthdate to a person and Momenthos shows how old they were when a moment happened. It needs both the person's birthdate and the moment's date.

## Writing suggestions

On devices with Apple Intelligence, you can ask for a cleaned-up version of what you wrote. You see the suggestion first and choose whether to use it. This runs on your device.

## Editing and deleting

Open a moment and use its actions to edit or delete it. Deleting a moment is permanent and also deletes its reflections.
